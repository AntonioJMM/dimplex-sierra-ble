# Hearth Signal

Hearth Signal treats warmth and connectivity as one quiet system: a stable architectural enclosure containing a living, asymmetrical pulse. Form is reduced to the minimum needed for recognition, with generous negative space allowing every contour to remain legible at very small scale. Geometry communicates structure; one organic gesture communicates life.

The palette balances deep mineral blue with ember orange and a restrained electric cyan. Warm and cool colors never compete for equal attention: heat remains the focal point, while connectivity appears as a precise secondary signal. Every color relationship is meticulously calibrated for contrast, accessibility, and consistent reproduction.

Scale follows a disciplined rhythm of nested boundaries, repeated line weights, and deliberate offsets. Curves soften the technical framework without making it playful or ornamental. The mark should feel painstakingly constructed by a master craftsperson, with optical corrections that matter even when they cannot be consciously seen.

Composition favors centered mass with one controlled point of asymmetry. The visual hierarchy moves from enclosure, to flame, to signal, expressing a local device that is both physical and connected without explanatory text. Nothing is decorative; every interval and alignment is the result of careful, expert refinement.

The final expression must remain crisp, calm, and durable rather than fashionable. It should appear to have been labored over through countless reductions, preserving only forms that survive at icon scale. Master-level execution is visible in clean silhouettes, balanced breathing room, and the confident absence of unnecessary detail.
