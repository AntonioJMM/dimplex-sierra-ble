# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, evidence, and behavior, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Protect other people's privacy and remove device or account identifiers.
- Treat heating and electrical safety concerns seriously.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, devices, or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate publication of private information, unsafe testing pressure, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and protect reporters from retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Do not use a public issue when doing so could expose a person or private information.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing the project.
